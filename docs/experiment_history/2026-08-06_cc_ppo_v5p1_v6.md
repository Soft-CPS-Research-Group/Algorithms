## V5.1 actuator-strength campaign

V5 showed two useful temporal schedules over the frozen PPO:
`community_export` reduced annual settled cost by EUR 33.69 and
`cheap_and_export` reduced it by EUR 31.85. Both passed the hard gates and
improved nearly every building, but increased ramping and battery throughput.

The price multiplier itself is not the only actuator parameter. When the CC
emits a discount, `SignalAwareRBCSmartLocal` charges at
`signal_price_charge_rate`. V5 used `0.60`; V5.1 tests `0.45` and `0.30` while
holding the temporal schedule, PPO checkpoint, seed, settlement and scorecard
contract fixed. This tests whether a weaker charge response retains cost gains
while reducing ramping and throughput.

The original A2 failed before Slurm submission during dataset materialisation
(`cp -a` over SSH). The same scientific configuration was resubmitted as A2
retry after A5 had successfully materialised the dataset. This is an
infrastructure failure, not a model result. A5 uses future trace information
and is diagnostic ceiling evidence only; it is never promotion-eligible.

Promotion remains scorecard-based, not cost-only: zero electrical violations,
EV/deferrable/SoC/outage gates, per-building settled cost, daily and all-time
peaks, ramping, load factor, import/export, emissions, solar self-consumption,
battery throughput, V2G and fairness are compared with the exact neutral PPO.

## Online V6

V5 schedules were trace-derived and therefore cannot be the final deployable
CC. V6 restores the intended hierarchy: one trainable online `CCLevel1` sees
only district aggregates and emits one scalar multiplier in `[0.5, 1.3]` every
four 15-minute steps. Seventeen frozen PPO actors retain their original local
observations; the signal reaches only their strict-local SMART residual base.

Three candidates isolate the CC reward weights while keeping the leaf and
actuator fixed at charge rate `0.45`:

| Candidate | Cost | Peak | Ramping | Export | Smoothness |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| cost first | 1.00 | 0.05 | 0.05 | 0.01 | 0.002 |
| balanced | 1.00 | 0.20 | 0.15 | 0.03 | 0.005 |
| peak guarded | 1.00 | 0.40 | 0.30 | 0.05 | 0.010 |

Each annual configuration uses six episodes: one full-year BC collection and
pretraining episode, four PPO learning episodes and one deterministic final
evaluation. The final deterministic episode no longer enters the rollout or
updates the CC weights.

The CC reward now supports `cost_aggregation=community_settled`. Its shared
settlement implementation mirrors CityLearn's weighted local-import
allocation, local buyer/seller prices and remaining grid import/export. This
aligns training with `community_settled_cost_total_eur`, including the case in
which members have different tariffs; the previous `community_net` formula was
only a proxy.

Reward references were calibrated from the matching annual neutral PPO job
`18649307-5dbf-4bee-96a5-47fb5ca3531a`: target import 5.742011 kWh, p90 settled
step cost EUR 1.483289, p90 excess-import squared 11.635773, p90 import ramp
1.632929 kWh and p90 export 4.878998 kWh.
